Microsoft Fabric consulting: Is it worth it in 2026?

Most companies don't have a use case strong enough for Fabric. And then they end up overpaying for something that has minimal impact on their growth. They're essentially building a cathedral to store a single candle.
‍

Why Fabric?

When a company stops treating data as a scattered byproduct and starts asking systemic, real-time questions about the business logic beneath the surface, they turn to Fabric.

This data layer takes all the relevant points from every system you use: CRM, ERP, accounting tools, product analytics, spreadsheets someone still lovingly maintains in Excel, APIs, logs and everything in between. Then it does 3 things to these data points: it brings them together, it cleans and shapes them into something usable, and delivers them to the right people at the right time.

On top of that, Fabric is a well-suited data platform for what we bring as an extra service: embedded analytics, working directly with Fabric APIs.

Why Stories of Data

There's a meaningful difference between a software development company that lists Power BI and Fabric as two lines in a services menu and a team that has been living inside Microsoft's analytics ecosystem since 2018.

We came up through Power BI in 2018 and have done wonderful things with it beyond graphs and charts. We learned that what companies actually want is to understand their own story, the why beyond it all, and face all situations with a better understanding of their business. Dracula, the renowned antihero, strikes from malice, but only those who are in the dark. We bring light and clarity to your projects.

We've been helping local (and international) companies rethink their processes and emerge more equipped to provide an exceptional product or service. And we'll build you a cathedral only if you truly need one.
